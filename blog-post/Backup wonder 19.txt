<h2 style="text-align: left;">History&nbsp;</h2>
<p>Commissioned by Emperor Vespasian of the Flavian dynasty in AD 72 and completed by his son Titus in AD 80, the Colosseum was designed to host grand spectacles, including gladiatorial contests, animal hunts, and public executions. This architectural marvel could accommodate between 50,000 to 80,000 spectators, who were seated according to social rank. The structure's dimensions are impressive, measuring approximately 189 meters long, 156 meters wide, and 50 meters high. Its outer wall is composed of travertine stone, while the interior features a complex system of vaults and arches made of concrete. <br />Beneath the arena lay the hypogeum, a network of tunnels and chambers where gladiators and animals awaited their turn in the spotlight. Despite suffering damage from earthquakes and centuries of neglect, the Colosseum has undergone extensive restoration efforts. Today, it stands as a UNESCO World Heritage Site and a powerful symbol of Rome's enduring legacy, offering visitors a chance to step back in time and explore the grandeur of the ancient Roman Empire.</p>

<h4>1. Leonardo da Vinci&ndash;Fiumicino Airport (FCO)</h4>
<p><strong>Location</strong>: Approximately 35 kilometers southwest of central Rome.<br /><strong>Overview</strong>: Fiumicino Airport is the primary international airport serving Rome and is the busiest airport in Italy. The airport features four terminals, with modern facilities and a wide range of services for travelers</p>
<h4>2. Ciampino&ndash;G.B. Pastine International Airport (CIA)</h4>
<p><strong>Location</strong>: Approximately 12 kilometers southeast of central Rome. <br /><strong>Overview</strong>: Ciampino Airport is the secondary airport serving Rome, mainly handling low-cost airlines and charter flights. It is closer to the city center compared to Fiumicino, making it a convenient option for travelers seeking quick access to Rome. Despite being smaller and less busy, Ciampino Airport provides essential services and amenities for passengers.</p>
