<h2><br />History Of The Wonder&nbsp;</h2>
<p>The history of the Sagrada Familia is as captivating as its design. Conceived in 1882, the basilica's construction began under architect Francisco de Paula del Villar before visionary Antoni Gaud&iacute; took over in 1883. Gaud&iacute; devoted his life to the project, blending Gothic and Art Nouveau styles, until his untimely death in 1926. Despite numerous interruptions, including the Spanish Civil War, work has continued, driven by private donations and public fascination. Today, the Sagrada Familia stands as a testament to Gaud&iacute;'s genius, with its completion projected for 2026, marking the centenary of Gaud&iacute;'s death.</p>

<h2>Here are some informative and concise fun facts about the Sagrada Familia:</h2>
<p><strong>1. Ongoing Construction:</strong> Construction began in 1882 and is expected to be completed by 2026, marking 144 years of continuous work.</p>
<p><strong>2. Architectural Style:</strong> Combines Gothic and Art Nouveau styles with innovative organic forms inspired by nature, designed by Antoni Gaud&iacute;.</p>
<p><strong>3. Symbolism:</strong> Every detail of the basilica's design symbolizes elements of the Christian faith and the natural world, reflecting Gaud&iacute;'s deep religious beliefs.</p>
<p><strong>4. Funding:</strong> Financed entirely by donations and ticket sales, making it one of the world's largest privately funded architectural projects.</p>
<p><strong>5. Impressive Height:</strong> Upon completion, it will reach 172 meters (564 feet), making it one of the tallest religious structures globally.</p>
